Professionals whose path inspires my project, met through My Job Glasses, LinkedIn or the N7 alumni network.

Role model 1 · My Job Glasses · 1-hour interview, Oct 2026

C. D.

Vice-President R&D @ Eddyfi Technologies (Québec, Canada)

Path
  1. ABB: scientist on a satellite instrument, then project manager and R&D director
  2. INO, then Telops: led about 150 optics researchers, then 10 years as R&D director
  3. Eddyfi Technologies: VP R&D for about 5 years, merging R&D teams after acquisitions
What I take away
Go technical first, then learn project management, systems engineering and innovation. With AI, value shifts to specifying needs and testing results.

Role model 2 · N7 alumni · Career & network talk at N7

L. C.

Technical Product Manager @ Amazon Fulfillment Technologies & Robotics (New York)

Path
  1. N7 (Electrical Engineering & Automation, 2017) and ÉTS Montréal; co-founded SmartSquare (smart-home IoT)
  2. Product Manager at OptumSoft (Menlo Park), then Stanley Robotics (Paris), 2017–2019
  3. Founded Balad (New York, 2020–2023), then robotics product roles at RightHand Robotics and Amazon
What I take away
Referrals beat job ads (1 in 3 vs 1 in 1,000). Build my network before I need an internship: reach out to engineers, not HR, with a short message and a precise time slot.
