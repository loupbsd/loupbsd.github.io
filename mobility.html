My three mobility projects

Options under study, not final choices.

Project A

Imperial College London

Type
Bilateral exchange, even semester (spring)
Why
Prestige (2nd in the QS World University Rankings 2025) and a British tech and motorsport network.
Possible courses
Imperial has no general engineering program: exchange students join a department. Possible picks: Computing: a mobile robotics course · Mechanical Engineering: Embedded C for Microcontrollers, Mechatronics · EEE: Control and optimisation, Sensor systems engineering.
Motorsport
Imperial Formula Racing (Formula Student, about 200 students from several departments, attached to mechanical engineering).
To confirm
The exact department of my agreement, and whether an exchange student can join the team.

Project B

Università di Bologna

Type
Erasmus exchange (spring)
Why
The heart of the Motor Valley (Ducati, Lamborghini, Ferrari, Maserati), and I am studying Italian.
Possible courses
Master's courses taught in English: Electronic Engineering for Intelligent Vehicles (Autonomous Driving Engineering track, designed with premium and racing manufacturers; courses such as Advanced automotive sensors, Connected vehicles, Deep learning for engineering applications) · Automation Engineering (robust control, software for real-time control, vision) · Electric Vehicle Engineering.
Motorsport
UniBo Motorsport (Formula SAE: a hybrid car, an electric car and an electric motorcycle, more than 250 students).
To confirm
Whether my Erasmus agreement lets me take these courses, and whether an exchange student can join the team.

Project C

Politecnico di Milano

Type
Erasmus exchange (spring)
Why
Automation and autonomous driving, a direct link with the automotive world. The Poli Moove team has won several editions of the Indy Autonomous Challenge.
Possible courses
Master's level, taught in English: Automation and control in autonomous vehicles · Automation and control in electric and hybrid vehicles · Software Engineering for automation.
To confirm
Whether my agreement (electronics and engineering fields) covers these courses.

My criteria

What I look for in an exchange semester.

Format
One 6-month exchange in year 2 (spring semester): in year 2, only an exchange is possible, not a double degree.
Language
Courses in English, French or Italian only: I cannot study in another language.
Culture
Real immersion, with an Anglo-Saxon or Italian work culture.
Field
Motorsport and automotive first, then embedded systems and software.
